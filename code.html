Meet the creator

Hi, I'm the maker behind Little Bot Creations! I'm 11 years old and I love 3D printing — designing new things, picking colors, and figuring out how to make ideas actually work in plastic.

Everything here is printed with kid-friendly PLA filament, which is made from cornstarch. It's safe to handle and play with — but sadly, it is not edible, so please don't take a bite.

Every order helps me buy filament, keep learning new designs, and try things I haven't printed before. Thanks for stopping by my shop!
